A thoughtful place to start

When comparing treatment options, focus on clear information about the approach, your dog’s individual needs, and guidance from a qualified veterinary professional.

01

Understand the terms

Ask the provider how they define each therapy and what the proposed treatment involves.

02

Consider your dog

Discuss your dog’s health, the reason treatment is being considered, and whether an option is appropriate.

03

Ask before deciding

Talk through expected benefits, potential risks, costs, and alternatives with your veterinarian.

A note for pet parents: This page is a starting point for questions, not medical advice. A veterinarian can provide recommendations for your individual dog.
