When your dog barks

The PetSmart guide discusses possible reasons for barking and offers these response ideas.

🐕

Notice the trigger

Look for the reason

The guide notes that dogs may bark for attention, excitement, or because they feel worried. Consider what is happening when the barking starts.

💙

Respond calmly

Keep your response steady

For attention-seeking barking, the article suggests waiting for quiet before offering attention. It also cautions that yelling may make the noise worse.

🐾

Build familiarity

Help ease worries

For barking linked to perceived threats, the guide recommends socialization and gradually helping dogs become more familiar with what worries them.
